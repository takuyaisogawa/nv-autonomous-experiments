# Human NV selection and characterization: September 2023

The [original laboratory log](NV1Log_2023_09.docx) records NV selection and
characterization performed by Takuya Isogawa on 16–18 September 2023. The task
was assigned by a senior researcher during Isogawa's second year of
doctoral study. It is the human reference example described in Supplementary
Note 4 of the accompanying manuscript.

## Purpose and selection

The search supported the experiments later reported by Hernández-Gómez et al.,
[*Interferometry of quantum correlation functions to access quasiprobability
distribution of work*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-024-00913-x),
**npj Quantum Information 10, 115 (2024)**.

The aim was to find an NV with long electron-spin coherence and no resolved
additional carbon-13 coupling in its Ramsey spectrum. The log records frequency
sweeps, Rabi calibrations, Ramsey measurements, and Fourier spectra.

The log states: “NV_8 is the best. NV_3 is OK.” Both candidates had clean,
single-line Ramsey spectra and long coherence times; NV_8 was preferred and
NV_3 was an acceptable alternative.
